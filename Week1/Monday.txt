### PowerPoint 9:30 AM

This is our presentation on exploring what roles look like in data. 

**Similarities and differences**

SQL and data concepts and understanding

Data science - AI and ML skills
Data analysis - business and stakeholder focus
Data engineering - purely behind the scenes instead of dealing with business.  

**Data Scientist** - Dealing with the future of data, predictions, answering business questions with data

- Maths background (understand maths behind coding)
- Projects (Data cleansing with artificial noise with various methods)
- AI (Machine Learning project on data prediction, and assessing importance of features to data),-
- Used to programming with intent to solve problems as part of coursework
- Technically challenging but eager to learn about new tools (SQL, etc.) as this is biggest downfall, technical knowhow. 
- Best of all options for me personally

**Data Engineer** - dealing with the background of data, the pipeline, cleansing and preparation of data for internal use

- Confidence translating python skills to SQL and more complex tasks
- Logical decomposition of complex problems due to project history is a key skill
- 4/5 due to current less interest in that style of work

**Data Analyst** - more focussed on business aspects of data, providing insights based on prepared data to give reasonable suggestions

- Communication is key when presenting, confident and comfortable in that environment
- Data analytic skills are a key component, easy for me to translate from my degree
- 3/5 due to lack of problem solving technical application beyond basic SQL that I thrive in and enjoy

Conclusion - Differences and preferences between each of us, but there are certain overarching skills we already share and will share by the end of the training that make us suitable for all these roles. Thanks a lot for listening.
