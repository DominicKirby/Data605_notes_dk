### Profile work

AI - watermarks in the text (American, M-dash) 

#### Main Properties 
- **Next token prediction** - vastly sophisticated autocomplete
- **Knowledge** - Training Data
- **Working memory** - What its prompted to account for
- **Steerability** - Continuing a pattern not an intent

**AI prompt manipulation** 
- Feed it more context to direct it into further detail
	- i.e. information about you 
	- Format of answer
	- Explain or demonstrate etc.
	- Amount of technical jargon 
	- i.e. technically limited knowledge 
- More specific the higher chance of yielding an answer you want
- Iterative nature
- Use an example of an output that you desire (1 shot prompting)
- Using a further example is (2 shot prompting) etc.
- "Give me an example of a think-first prompt using the previous scenario"
- Chain prompting - multiple in one

**Data Privacy** - Focus on this first
 - Anonymise where necessary
 - Remove company data, names, places, etc.

**AI Fluency Framework (4D's)**
- Delegation - setting goals and deciding whether to engage with AI.
- Description - Effectively describing goals to prompt useful behaviour and output.
- Discernment - Accurate assessment of the usefulness of the AI outputs and behaviours.
- Diligence - Take responsibility for what we do with AI and how we do it.

**Tools for automating pipelines**
4 Categories: 
- Workflow Orchestrators - task management, error recovery, monitoring

- Ingestion and Extraction tools - automate the movement from databases into lakes and warehouses
	- Fivetran - low maintenance with pre-built connectors 
- Data Transformation tools - automate SQL and PySpark data modelling
	- dbt - industry standard for internal to the warehouse
- CI/CD, quality and infrastructure automation - code lifecycle, infrastructure, reliability testing
	- Terrafrom - automatically provision warehouse and cloud storage
