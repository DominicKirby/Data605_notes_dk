#### Projects and Stakeholders
- **BAU activities vs Project Work**
- **Programme vs Individual Projects**
- **Stages & Progression of the project lifecycle**
- **Role of stakeholders in projects and stakeholder management**


**Business as Usual vs. Projects**-
- BAU - normal execution of standard operations within an organisation
	- IT help desk tickets
- Projects - unique and transient with a desired outcome
	- Implementation of a new CRM system

**Programme**
- Overseas several projects not a single one
- Can span several years

**Project Lifecycle**
- **1.** Initiation - Deciding on the project, objectives, requirements
- **2.** Planning - Assessing tools and workflows
- **3.** Executing - Implementing the project workflow
- **4.** Monitoring and Controlling - Performance metrics
- **5.** Closing - Formal acceptance, lessons learned, reviews

## Week 1 Assessment Content

### Stakeholders and Balancing Resources
#### Triple Constraint to balance Quality 
- Time - Project Length  
	- Rushing -> Mistakes
	- Extra time -> Revisions
- Cost - Price of the Project -
	- Low Budget -> reduced resource quality and scope width
	- High Budget - Better resources
- Scope - Amount of work/ tasks
	- Narrower scope -> better quality
	- More time required, Larger scope -> spread too thinly
All managed to ensure good quality

#### POPIT - Holistic Triangle 
Assess wider implications across company
			
			Organisation
			Technology
		People           Process

Organisation - New Roles may be required
Technology - New Tech Stacks and new technology use
People - Training required? Who will be using it?
Process - Change to methods used.

### Stakeholder Management 
**Initiation Phase**
- **Identify stakeholders** - *anyone* that would see impact from the project
- **Analyse stakeholders** - assess *influence, requirements, interest*
- **Devise stakeholder management strategies** - build strategies tailoring to their requirements interests and needs, also using different *levels of communication*
**Execution Phase** 
- **Review** - revise strategies and monitor stakeholders

#### Power/ Influence Interest grid

**High**        | Watch | Satisfy |Constant| 
**Some**      |         Keep Onside             |
**None**       | Ignore |  Keep Informed  |
		|  **None |  Some   |   High**   | 


#### Stakeholder attitudes
- **Champion** - Actively working for the success for the project, funding, bring following
- **Supporter** - Not active, in favour of the project
- **Neutral** - No opinion for or against
- **Critic** - Not in favour of the project but not actively against it
- **Opponent** - Actively works to obstruct a project, without directly obstructing progress
- **Blocker** - Obstructs progress, physical barrier

#### Making Projects easier
- **Positive** atmosphere
- Manage conflict **intelligently** 
- Open and clear **communication**
- **Respect** all peers
- **Trustworthiness**
